# 4. Families and relations

A family is not necessarily a complete theory. It may be:

- an executable rule;
- a successor with a different prescription;
- an obstruction;
- a classification;
- a bibliographic antecedent;
- an architecture that still lacks a bridge.

## Four common relations

### Subclass

F14 imposes `s=2`, `Z^d`, and isotropy within the broader class of causal
particles in F13. The conclusion of F14 does not classify all of F13.

### Shared support

F12, F14, F16, and F17 use or study related BCC/Weyl structures. This does not
mean that they have the same operator or objective: composite Maxwell theory,
classification, an explicit rule, and momentum symmetry are different tasks.

### Restricted obstruction

F5 excludes a specific factorization. It does not exclude a grouped circuit,
ancilla, or supercell outside its quantifiers.

### Architectural complement

F8, F9, F10, and F11 contribute different pieces concerning constraints,
gauge, links, and currents. Combining them in one sentence does not
automatically construct a joint unitary rule.
